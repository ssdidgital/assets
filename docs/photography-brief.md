# Photography brief: System Switch

**Purpose:** replace the portrait placeholders on the homepage, About and Insights, and give the site, share cards and decks a consistent photographic language. The design is square, flat and editorial; the photography should feel like the same hand made it.

## Feel
Calm, exact, unhurried. A closer at work, not a guru on stage. Natural light, real rooms, nothing staged to look busy. If a frame could appear in any agency's "about us" page, it's the wrong frame.

## Palette and grade
- Grounds that sit with the brand: warm off-white walls (close to Linen `#f5f3ec`), stone and oatmeal fabrics, deep green (close to Forest `#11352b`) through a jacket, a chair, a wall or foliage.
- Gold only as an accent if it occurs naturally (brass, a lamp, warm window light). Never props bought to match.
- Grade warm and slightly matte. Lifted blacks, no teal-and-orange, no heavy vignette, no blur effects. Skin true to life.

## Shot list

|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Founder portrait, hero.** Waist-up, three-quarter turn, looking at camera, plain warm wall. Two expressions: neutral-confident and slight smile. | Homepage founder section, About | 4:5 (deliver 2400 × 3000) |

| 6 | **Environment.** The room itself, empty, morning light. | Spare backgrounds for slides | 16:9 |

## Wardrobe
Solid, matte fabrics: forest green, navy, stone, cream, charcoal. No logos, no busy patterns, no shiny materials. Two changes minimum, one of them a jacket.

## Do not
- Stock photography, AI-generated images, or composites.
- Pointing at screens, arms crossed, "thinking" poses, laughing at salads.
- Rounded crops, drop shadows, duotone filters or overlays in post. Crops are square-cornered, like the rest of the system.

## Delivery
- RAW plus edited full-resolution JPEG (sRGB), at the sizes above, with 10% bleed for crops.
- Filenames: `ssd-[shot#]-[short-name]-[nn].jpg`.
- Model release for the founder; property release for any location.
- Usage: web, social, print and paid ads, worldwide, in perpetuity.
